🚘 Vehicle

🛣️ Drive tips

  • Leave Pune by 6 AM to clear the ghats in daylight; aim to reach Goa before dark.
  • Pune → Satara → Kolhapur on NH48 is the easy stretch; ghat section after that — go slow in rain.
  • Refuel in Kolhapur/Belagavi side before the ghats; Goa pumps get crowded in the evening.
  • Keep FASTag topped up; tolls are auto-added to the estimate below.
  • Goa: don't drink & drive — cab or designated driver on BAAREE / UNVRS / LPK nights.
  • Parking at Baga/Calangute and Vagator fills up after 5 PM — go early for sunsets.
