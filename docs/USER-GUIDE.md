# 📱 How to use the trip planner (2-minute read)

**First time:** open the link Akshay sent. Pick **your name** and enter the **trip passcode**.
Then add it to your home screen:
- iPhone: open it in Safari, then *Share → Add to Home Screen*.
- Android: in Chrome, tap *⋮ → Add to Home screen*.

The pill at the top shows the sync status:

| Pill | Meaning |
|---|---|
| ✅ Synced | Everyone sees the same thing. |
| 🕓 Waiting / ⏳ Saving | Your change is on its way. |
| 📴 Offline | No signal. Your changes are saved on your phone and upload by themselves later. |
| ⚠️ / 🔒 | Tap the pill, and check the passcode. |

## Add a bill 🧾
1. In **🗓️ Plan**, find the slot (e.g. *26 Sep · 12–2 PM*) and tap **＋ bill** under it.
2. Type the **amount** and **where / what** (e.g. *Lunch at Britto's – 2 thalis*). The category is guessed for you. Tap **✓ Save bill**.
3. The slot now shows 🧾 ₹ with the amount. Tap it to change who paid, how it's split, or to delete it.

Other ways to add a bill: **💰 Where the money went** (under the plan) or the **Budget** tab.
The split is automatic: you two for 25–28 Sep, all 5 from 29 Sep. You can change it on any bill.

## Add or change a place 📍
- Tap a slot and type, e.g. `🍽️ Dinner at Thalassa`. When you tap away, **📍 Thalassa** appears, and it opens in Google Maps.
- Or **paste a Google Maps link** into the slot, straight from Maps → *Share → Copy link*.
- **⋯** on a slot opens a map preview, a note field, merge/split, all its bills and **🔎 Reviews & better options nearby**.

## "Should I go?" 🔎
Open **🔎 Explore & Reviews**, then type a name or paste a Maps link:
- You get a **0–10 score** and a verdict (✅ Go · 👍 Good pick · 🤔 Only if nearby · ⛔ Skip), with the newest reviews.
- Pick the **day and time slot**, then tap **📍 Use my current location**. It shows **better-rated places near you**, and **Use this** adds one to the plan.

## Oops, something got deleted 😬
**🕘 History** holds cloud snapshots (every 2 hours) and restore points on your phone.
Tap **Preview → Restore this version**. Nothing is lost: the restore can be undone the same way.

## Tips
- Two people can edit at the same time. Both changes are kept.
- The plan checks for other people's changes every 30 seconds, and whenever you reopen the app.
- Late September is the end of the monsoon: check that a place is open (⚡ Live check) before driving out.
